# Payments should be invisible.

We started Ariapay because every payment tool we tried felt like it was designed for the company, not the customer. Slow, opaque, and full of friction. We built the opposite.

## Our mission

Make commerce faster for every business, everywhere — from the corner café to the enterprise retailer.

## What we stand for

### Uncompromising speed

Setiap milidetik penting saat checkout. Kami terobsesi dengan latensi agar pelangganmu tidak pernah menunggu.

### Security as default

* PCI DSS Level 1 certified
* End-to-end encryption
* Fraud checking on every transaction

### Radical transparency

* Flat pricing
* Real-time dashboard
* Zero hidden fees — what you see is what you pay.

### Built for builders

* Developer-first APIs
* Clear documentation
* SDKs that get you to your first payment in minutes.

## The story

Founded in 2022 by a team of fintech engineers and ex-banking operators, Ariapay was built from a single frustration: accepting a payment should never be harder than making one.

Today we process over 2 million transactions a month for more than 500 merchants across retail, hospitality, and professional services.

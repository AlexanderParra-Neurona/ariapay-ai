# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5/12/2026

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, phone number, and business details when you register an account, and payment card information when you add a payment method.

We also collect information automatically, including device identifiers, IP address, transaction history, and usage data, to operate and secure the platform.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Create and manage your Ariapay account
- Process transactions and prevent fraud
- Provide customer support and respond to inquiries
- Comply with legal and regulatory obligations
- Improve and secure our products and services

## 3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with payment networks, banking partners, regulators, and service providers who need it to deliver the service, and only to the extent necessary.

We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Ariapay, our users, or the public.

## 4. Data Security

Card numbers and other sensitive data are encrypted at rest and in transit using industry-standard encryption (AES-256). We are PCI DSS Level 1 certified and undergo regular independent security audits.

CVV codes are never stored after a transaction is authorised, and card numbers are shown masked everywhere in our app and dashboard.

## 5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active, and for a period afterward as required to comply with legal, tax, and regulatory obligations, or to resolve disputes.

If you delete your account, we soft-delete your data and permanently erase it after the legally required retention period has elapsed.

## 6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing.

You can update your profile information directly in the app, or request account deletion at any time from Settings or via our account-deletion portal.

## 7. Cookies and Tracking

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, and understand how our site is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Ariapay is not intended for use by anyone under 18 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes through the app or by email before they take effect.

## 10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, contact us at privacy@ariapay.com.
